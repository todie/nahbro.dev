nahbro.dev

nah bro as a service

there are a lot of robots.

they want your content.

nahbro.dev generates the files that ask them to leave.

curl -s https://nahbro.dev/robots.txt > public/robots.txt curl -s https://nahbro.dev/llms.txt > public/llms.txt

GPTBot. ClaudeBot. Bytespider. CCBot. all of them.

the list is in the source. when a new one shows up, send a PR.

some of them will listen.

the rest will not.

the files are here either way.

nahbro.dev serves its own robots.txt. obviously.
