nahbro.dev

tell LLMs no. serve the files that make AI crawlers behave.

// what

A dead-simple API that generates robots.txt and llms.txt files pre-loaded with every known AI crawler user-agent. Drop them at the root of your site and stop feeding the machine — or at least make it ask nicely.

// add to your site
robots.txt — block all AI crawlers
curl -o public/robots.txt https://nahbro.dev/robots.txt
llms.txt — signal your content preferences
curl -o public/llms.txt https://nahbro.dev/llms.txt
or fetch both at once
curl -o public/robots.txt https://nahbro.dev/robots.txt -o public/llms.txt https://nahbro.dev/llms.txt
